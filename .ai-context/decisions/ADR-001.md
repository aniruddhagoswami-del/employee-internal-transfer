# ADR-001: Orchestration (SAGA) vs Choreography for Downstream Systems

## Status
Approved

## Date
2026-09-04

## Context
Four disparate enterprise systems (Core HRIS, Global Payroll, IT IAM, and Facilities CAFM) require coordinated, consistent updates upon employee transfer approval. The transfer journey demands full transparency and live SLA tracking for all stakeholders.

## Decision
Adopt **Centralized Orchestration SAGA** with a Transactional Outbox pattern rather than decentralized choreography.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** Single point of state tracking; real-time visibility into each adapter's execution state; simplified error handling and manual intervention workflows.
- **Negative / Risks:** Orchestrator becomes a critical coordination component; requires idempotent adapters and retry management.
