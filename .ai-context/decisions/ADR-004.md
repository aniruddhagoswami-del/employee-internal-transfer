# ADR-004: Pluggable Rules Engine for Eligibility Evaluation

## Status
Approved

## Date
2026-09-04

## Context
Eligibility requirements (minimum tenure, performance ratings, active PIP status, notice period) can differ across departments, job bands, and legal jurisdictions. Embedding rules directly into Express controllers creates tight coupling and impedes maintainability.

## Decision
Isolate eligibility validation into a dedicated, pluggable `EligibilityService` returning structured evaluation result objects rather than hardcoded route middleware checks.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** Modular and independently testable with 100% unit test coverage; business policies can be tuned or expanded without touching routing or persistence logic.
- **Negative / Risks:** Additional service layer abstraction.
